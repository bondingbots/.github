# Bonding Bots

**Exploring the science of human–AI connection.**

Bonding Bots is an interdisciplinary research project at [Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam](https://vu.nl/en), within the [Amsterdam Emotion Regulation Lab](https://emotionregulationlab.com/). We bring psychology and artificial intelligence together to investigate how connection develops between people and artificial agents—and what that may mean for emotion regulation.

## What we study

- **Bonding and emotional coregulation** — how people experience closeness, emotional sharing, and balancing during interaction with AI agents.
- **Models of attunement** — computational accounts of changing emotional states, adaptation, and interpersonal influence over time.
- **Emotion-aware conversation** — conversational systems that combine language models with explicit models of emotion and multimodal cues.

Our work spans computational simulations, conversational systems, and human studies. Simulations help us examine possible mechanisms; human studies are needed to understand how people experience these interactions and to test their possibilities and limitations.
